# Final Presentation Outline (8–12 slides)

1. **Problem and objectives** — laundry order, payment, and status tracking.
2. **System overview** — Python/Tkinter, MySQL/XAMPP, and the three roles.
3. **ERD** — entities, keys, junction table, and service hierarchy.
4. **Normalization** — dependencies and progression to 3NF/BCNF.
5. **Application workflow** — login, customer, order, payment, and status screens.
6. **Advanced SQL** — procedures, triggers, views, function, joins, aggregates,
   and CTE.
7. **Transactions and concurrency** — payment locking, rollback, and ACID.
8. **Security** — PBKDF2 passwords, Fernet contact encryption, prepared
   statements, roles, and audit log.
9. **Reporting and performance** — sales report, indexes, and `EXPLAIN`
   comparison.
10. **Backup, recovery, and ethics** — XAMPP restore test, privacy risks, and
    Data Privacy Act considerations.
11. **Demo results** — successful CRUD, role restrictions, injection test, and
    recovery evidence.
12. **Lessons learned and future scale** — warehouse star schema and dashboard.
